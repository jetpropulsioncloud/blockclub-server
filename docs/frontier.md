# Frontier

## Purpose
The Frontier is BlockClub's rotating competitive/resource campaign world.

## Current campaign model
- Campaign duration: 120 hours
- Guilds compete through contribution and territory growth
- Winning guild receives Guild Points / campaign rewards
- Camps are pasted into the Frontier
- Frontier resets between campaigns
